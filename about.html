About Bedhill Trading

A 100% black-owned construction and project management company, founded in 2009.

Our story

Built on quantity surveying and project management

Bedhill Trading CC was founded in 2009 by Mr A. Chiku, a qualified quantity surveyor and project manager who has worked in the construction industry in provinces across South Africa.

The company is graded 4GB and 4CE on the CIDB grading system and is wholly owned by historically disadvantaged individuals. It has earned its place among South Africa's emerging construction and project management companies, and has been joined by four more experienced senior technical staff who add to its skills.
