How a project runs

The same steps whether it's a classroom block or a home extension.

  1. Site visit and quote

    We look at the site, agree the scope and price the work.

  2. Plan and programme

    Materials, teams and timelines are set before work starts.

  3. Build

    Our team builds and supervises the site, with regular progress updates.

  4. Handover

    We inspect the work with you and hand over a finished building.
